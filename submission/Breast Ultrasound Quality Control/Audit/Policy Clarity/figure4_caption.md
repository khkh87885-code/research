Risk-ranked review curves and external paired and matched-count comparisons. Panels A and B show empirical step curves of the fraction of low-quality patients detected versus the actual number selected for simulated review, ranked by descending risk with official patient identifier strings as the lexicographic tie-breaker. The cohorts contain 93/851 and 16/252 low-quality patients, respectively (Dice <0.70). Blue solid and orange dashed curves represent Methods A and B. Filled squares mark the development-locked cutoffs (internal A: 71/93 detected at 174 reviews; B: 68/93 at 150; external A: 10/16 at 35; B: 13/16 at 33). Open circles mark 50 external reviews per method (A: 14/16; B: 13/16). Panel C retains the original paired nominal 95% Newcombe method 10 intervals: +18.75 [−4.28, +40.04] percentage points at locked cutoffs and −6.25 [−31.61, +19.57] at top 50. The latter interval conditions on the observed rankings and does not account for re-ranking in another sample. Panel D is a post hoc descriptive display of counts at the same K (33, 35 or 50) per method, extracted from the external curves for the same 16 low-quality patients; no new intervals or hypothesis tests are introduced. K33 and K35 correspond to the observed locked-cutoff review counts, while K50 repeats the original protocol's supplementary budget. The different order at K50 versus K33/K35 illustrates budget dependence within this cohort.

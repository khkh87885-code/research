75	Normal	Table S12. Post hoc diagnostic-stratum symmetric Kitagawa decomposition of external-minus-internal differences.
76	Normal	Post hoc descriptive accounting at the locked 20 %-development-budget thresholds (A=0.13705134391784668; B=0.06972856653991777), without recalibration. Direction is external minus internal. Strata are dataset-reported benign and malignant diagnoses; not every diagnosis was histopathologically confirmed.
77	Normal	Symmetric Kitagawa identity: overall difference = Σ[(wE−wI)(pE+pI)/2] + Σ[(pE−pI)(wE+wI)/2]. Detection weights are the diagnostic proportions among low-quality cases; review weights are the diagnostic proportions among all patients. The first sum is composition-associated and the second is the within-stratum term. Weights are recomputed in every draw.
78	Normal	Intervals are nominal, unadjusted descriptive percentile 95 % intervals from 5,000 independent within-cohort patient bootstrap replicates (seed 20261107; NumPy PCG64; linear finite-value quantiles); A/B, quality and diagnosis remain paired. Duplicate draws are repeated observations of existing patients, not new cases.
79	Normal	Overall detection differences have 5,000 valid replicates. One draw has no external benign low-quality case, making its detection stratum rate undefined; the NA propagates to both detection components, leaving 4,999 valid component replicates. Review-fraction components and overall differences have 5,000 valid replicates. Undefined rates are not imputed even when their stratum weight is zero.
80	Normal	Two diagnostic strata cannot remove device, acquisition, annotation or other confounding. This decomposition is not a causal attribution and no percent-explained claim is made. Percentile intervals supplement the original H1–H3 Newcombe intervals; crossing zero does not demonstrate equivalence.
81	Normal	For diagnostic stratum s, C = Σ(w_E,s−w_I,s)(p_E,s+p_I,s)/2 and W = Σ(p_E,s−p_I,s)(w_E,s+w_I,s)/2, so D=C+W. Common weights w*=(w_E+w_I)/2 give a standardized external-minus-internal difference equal to W. Components may not sum to the displayed total after rounding. Method: Kitagawa EM, J Am Stat Assoc 1955;50:1168–1194, doi:10.1080/01621459.1955.10501299.

The correction concerns an ambiguity in **method 11** of the independent-proportions article (1998;17:873–890), p. 876. Method 10 gives two equivalent expressions for each of δ and ε. For method 11 those expressions are not equivalent and only the first should be used:

δ = sqrt{(a/m − l1)^2 + (u2 − b/n)^2}

ε = sqrt{(u1 − a/m)^2 + (b/n − l2)^2}

with l1, l2, u1 and u2 as specified for method 11. The correction explicitly states that the related paired-difference article (pp. 2635–2650), methods 8–10 (p. 2639), gives a single expression per quantity so no ambiguity arises.

**Manuscript implication:** The present independent contrasts use method 10, and the paired contrast uses the paired-difference method 10. This correction does not identify an error in either chosen method. The separate numerical audit remains the relevant validation of implementation and archived results; do not alter confidence intervals merely because an erratum exists.
